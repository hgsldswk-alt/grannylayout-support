Help

  1. Lay your squares out in rows on the floor, tap Photo a new blanket and take one photo from above. If the yellow lines miss the gaps, adjust the rows and columns, then tap Done.
  2. On the canvas, tap one square and then another to swap them. Long-press a square to lock it in place.
  3. Tap Smart shuffle to rearrange the unlocked squares so look-alike colors don't touch. If you have many squares of one color, a few look-alike pairs may remain.
  4. Tap Join to see the joining order row by row; tap once for each square you join.
  5. Export shares a preview image and a printable row-by-row PDF. The ••• menu lets you copy or paste a layout code.

使用帮助

  1. 把方块在地上摆成行列，点「拍一张新毯子」，站高一点俯拍一张。黄线没对准缝就加减行数列数，再点完成。
  2. 在画布上点一块再点另一块就互换；长按某块可锁定位置。
  3. 点「智能打乱」重排未锁定的方块，让颜色太像的不相邻。同一颜色太多时可能还剩少数几处。
  4. 点「开始拼」逐行查看拼接顺序，拼好一块点一下。
  5. 「导出」分享成品预览图和可打印的逐行顺序 PDF；右上角 ••• 菜单可复制或粘贴排版口令。

使用說明

  1. 把方塊在地上擺成行列，點「拍一張新毯子」，站高一點俯拍一張。黃線沒對準縫就加減列數欄數，再點完成。
  2. 在畫布上點一塊再點另一塊就互換；長按某塊可鎖定位置。
  3. 點「智慧打亂」重排未鎖定的方塊，讓顏色太像的不相鄰。同一顏色太多時可能還剩少數幾處。
  4. 點「開始拼」逐列查看拼接順序，拼好一塊點一下。
  5. 「匯出」分享成品預覽圖和可列印的逐列順序 PDF；右上角 ••• 選單可複製或貼上排版口令。

Privacy / 隐私 / 隱私

Granny Square Layout does not collect any data. Photos and layouts are stored only on your iPhone. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or servers. When you tap “Photo a new blanket” or “+ Square,” the app uses the camera to photograph your laid-out granny squares for a new layout or a single square to add to an existing one, and it processes and stores the images on your iPhone without automatically uploading them.
